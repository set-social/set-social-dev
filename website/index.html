Whoop, in depth

Connect it once from Settings → Integrations and Arnold has real-time recovery, sleep, and strain — no manual entry, ever. It shows up three places: a live snapshot on Home, full rings on your Stats tab, and directly in the numbers Arnold references when you ask how you're doing or what to train today. Low recovery nudges toward lighter work or extra rest; high recovery is a green light to push. You control the connection, and what's shared, any time.
